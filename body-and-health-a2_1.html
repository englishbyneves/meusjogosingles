FocusSpeaking + writing · sentences with because / so / but.
Success criteriaStudent says 5+ symptom sentences and writes 3+ correct pieces of advice.
Warm-up

Quick start Speaking · 2 min

Answer out loud, full sentences — no thinking time:

  1. How do you feel today, from 1 to 10? Why?
  2. When did you last go to a doctor or pharmacy? What happened?
  3. Point at your body — say 3 parts you can see right now.

Grammar reference (teacher) — how it works

My + part + hurts · I have / I've got a + ...ache · a sore + part.

should / shouldn't + base verb for advice. Connectors: because (reason), so (result), but (contrast).

Writing · type & check

Complete the sentences Writing · 4 min

Fill each gap with because, so, but, should or shouldn't. Then press Check.

Reading + writing

Spot the mistake Writing · 4 min

Each sentence has ONE mistake. Rewrite it correctly, then reveal the answer.

Guided practice

Match & react Speaking · 3 min

Teacher says a body part → student makes a problem + advice. "throat" → "I have a sore throat, so you should drink tea." Run fast, note errors for the end.

Writing · your own words

Give the advice Writing · 5 min

Read each problem. Write advice using should / shouldn't. Two ideas each.

1. "I have a bad cold." → You should stay home and drink hot tea. You shouldn't go out in the cold.

2. "My back hurts a lot." → You should rest and see a doctor. You shouldn't lift heavy things.

3. "I'm always tired." → You should sleep more. You shouldn't use your phone at night.

Writing · level up to A2

Connect it Writing · 4 min

Join the two ideas into ONE sentence with because, so or but.

1. I have a headache so I can't study.

2. She has a fever because she is sick.

3. My throat hurts but I feel OK.

Interactive · put it in order

Order the conversation Reading · 3 min

Use the arrows to put the clinic conversation in the right order, then check.

Main activity · forced fluency

At the clinic — react & advise Speaking

Tap New patient. The student gets a symptom and must talk for the full time — no stopping.

Tap “New patient” to start
  • React: "Oh no! / I'm sorry to hear that."
  • Ask 2 questions: "How long have you had it? / Do you have a fever?"
  • Give 2 pieces of advice with should / shouldn't.
  • End it: "Get well soon!"
—
Free speaking · swap roles

Real talk Speaking · 60 sec each

No script. Talk continuously for 60 seconds on ONE of these:

  1. The last time you were sick — what happened and what did you do?
  2. Your healthy (or unhealthy!) habits — and what you should change.
  3. Advice for a friend who never sleeps and always feels tired.
Homework

Practice at home

  1. Speaking: record a 45-sec voice note describing how you feel today + advice for yourself.
  2. Writing: a friend texts "I don't feel well 😷". Write a 4-line reply: ask what's wrong, give 2 pieces of advice (should/shouldn't), and use because or so once.
Did it work?

Success check

The lesson worked if the student can, without help: describe a problem ("My ___ hurts / I have a ___"), give advice with should/shouldn't, join ideas with because/so/but, and write a short reply to a sick friend.
