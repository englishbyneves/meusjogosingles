Talking about results

~9 min

In Britain, "0" in a score is nil. "Two–nil", "one–all". Read these real 2026 results out loud.

Germany7–1Curaçao

Say it: "Germany thrashed Curaçao seven–one."  /  "Germany hammered them."

Brazil3–0Scotland

Say it: "Brazil beat Scotland three–nil."  /  "Scotland crashed out."

Brazil1–1Morocco

Say it: "Brazil drew one–all with Morocco."  /  "It ended in a draw."

Canada6–0Qatar

Say it: "Canada thrashed Qatar six–nil at home."

React like a native: "What a result!" · "I can't believe it." · "They were robbed!" · "That was never a penalty." · "He was miles offside." · "Brilliant goal, though."
Speaking drill
Read all four results out loud, then react to each one with a phrase. Don't pause between them.

Opinions & predictions

~10 min

Real conversations need your view. Steal these — they're how British people actually talk.

Giving an opinion

"I reckon…" (very British = I think)

"If you ask me…"

"I'd say…"

"To be honest…"

Making a prediction

"My money's on Brazil."

"They've got a good chance of winning."

"There's no chance they'll win."

"It could go either way."

"Who'll win the World Cup?" → "To be honest, my money's on Brazil — they've got a good chance now Neymar's back. But I reckon France could go all the way too. England? There's no chance, if you ask me!"

Main activity — Be the pundit

~13 min

You're a football pundit on a British TV show. Two segments, straight through. The clock is your audience — keep talking till it stops.

Segment 1 · Match preview

Preview tonight's match for the viewers: Türkiye vs the USA. Who's the favourite? What do both teams need? What's your prediction?

"Good evening. Tonight we've got a huge match — Türkiye against the hosts, the USA. If you ask me, the Americans are the favourites, but Türkiye are dangerous on the counter-attack. My prediction? A 2–1 win for the USA…"
On air
Deliver your preview. No notes.

Segment 2 · The big story

React to the headline of the tournament: Cristiano Ronaldo became the first player ever to score in six different World Cups. Is he the greatest of all time? Agree or disagree — and say why.

For: "There's no doubt he's one of the best ever." · "Nobody has done what he's done."
Against: "I'd say Messi is still better." · "Records aren't everything, are they?"
On air
Give your take. Take a side and defend it.

Grammar in context — quick reference

Present perfect for tournament progress (no specific time): "Brazil have won two matches." · "Have you ever been to a match?" · "He's scored in six World Cups."

Past simple for finished, dated results: "Germany thrashed Curaçao on Sunday." · "Scotland lost 3–0 yesterday."

Predictions: will (sure) · might / could (possible) · going to (evidence now). "They will win." vs "They might win." vs "Look — they're going to score!"

Comparatives & superlatives: "Brazil are stronger than Scotland." · "That was the most exciting match of the group stage."
