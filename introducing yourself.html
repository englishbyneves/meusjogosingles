Quick tips (open only if needed)

Good evening is a greeting at night; good night is a goodbye.

For age, say I'm 30 (not "I have 30").

For hobbies, add -ing: cooking, playing, watching.

4 Talk to three people

Introduce yourself out loud to each one — and ask them one question back. Change your greeting to fit the time of day!

🌅 A new coworker
8 a.m. at the office
"Good morning! My name is…" + ask one thing
🏡 Your new neighbor
Saturday afternoon
"Hi! Nice to meet you…" + ask one thing
🎉 Someone at a party
9 p.m.
"Good evening! I'm…" + ask one thing
